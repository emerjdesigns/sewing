About me

A maker at heart

"Every stitch tells a story — I'm just here to help you tell yours."

Hi, I'm the maker behind EmerJDesigns! I've been sewing, embroidering, and crafting since I can remember. What started as a hobby quickly became a passion — and now I get to create meaningful, handmade pieces for people like you.

Whether it's a custom stuffed animal for a little one, embroidered personalisation on your favourite garment, or a piece of clothing made just for your body — every order receives the same love and attention to detail.
